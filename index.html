How to play

  • 2 to 4 players. Everyone gets 5 cards each round from a 21-card deck.
  • Each round has a table rank (Ace, King or Queen). On your turn, play 1 to 3 cards face-down and say they all match it.
  • Jokers are wild. The Devil can hide in any play.
  • Don't believe the last play? Call LIAR on your turn. The cards flip. If the claim was true, you pull the trigger. If it was a bluff, the player who played pulls it.
  • If a Devil is revealed, its owner is safe and everyone else pulls the trigger.
  • Playing your last card when only one other player holds cards forces an automatic LIAR.
  • Each player has one bullet hidden in a six-chamber gun, and the chamber never resets. Last player alive wins.
  • You have 45 seconds per turn. Miss two in a row and you're out.
